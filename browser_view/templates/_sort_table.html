{# Click-to-sort tables, shared by the View pages. sortableTable(table) wires each header's button.sort and returns sortBy(index). A column with data-type="num" sorts by its cells' data-sort; others by text. A blank cell (no value, or "–") is the lowest value, so it moves with the direction: last high-to-low, first low-to-high. A table with two header rows numbers its sortable headers with data-col, the body cell index each sorts (indices may skip a cell with no sort, such as a folded column); otherwise they are the first header row's cells, in order. sortBy(index) takes that body cell index. #}
